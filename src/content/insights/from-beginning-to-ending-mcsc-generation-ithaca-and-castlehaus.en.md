If we take care of the ending, who is responsible for our beginnings? **Gian Marco Gilardi**, Founder and Managing Partner of MCSC Group, on how the Group accompanies clients across every stage of their journey:

> *If we consider and take care of the ending, who is responsible for our beginnings?*
>
> In every story, to have an ending we first need a beginning — or else, what kind of story would it be?
>
> MCSC’s role in the lives of its clients is to strategically accompany them throughout their personal, professional and entrepreneurial journeys. With a multi-family office approach, it supports ultra-high-net-worth individuals and international families, entrepreneurs and industrial groups, as well as talent and brands across sport, fashion and entertainment — bringing together private wealth and family affairs, business interests and careers. From coordinating trusted advisers and planning succession to supporting business growth, strategic partnerships and career transitions, MCSC helps clients connect today’s decisions with their long-term ambitions.
>
> **Generation Ithaca** reinforces this approach through scenario planning, helping clients explore how major life, career and business decisions could shape their wealth, liquidity and future plans.
>
> **CastleHaus**, on the other hand, is the launching platform. Think about it in terms of a timeline: both are essential to one another, as together they complete a roadmap from start to end.
>
> CastleHaus is the incubator, mainly focused on artistic ventures. Its responsibility is to ensure the visibility and exposure of its clients in an oversaturated creative realm. Thanks to long and meaningful relationships sustained over the years, it builds bridges between those who stand high in the industry and those who have yet to leave a mark. As the name suggests, its ambitions are grand, while still providing the warmth and familiarity that so many creative agencies lack nowadays.
>
> Aside from its representative duties, **CastleHaus** also provides the brand strategy and artistic direction that clients may need in the post-career sphere — because a good brand exists long before its first product does. Exhibitions, exposure, editorial opportunities and collaborations: CastleHaus makes it happen. It is where our thoughts converge.
>
> Take the leap. MCSC’s ever-evolving ecosystem is designed to lift you higher, opening new doors and propelling you towards greater heights.
>
> *Ideas may be what starts us, but your trust is what keeps us moving.*

— **Gian Marco Gilardi**, Founder & Managing Partner, MCSC Group
