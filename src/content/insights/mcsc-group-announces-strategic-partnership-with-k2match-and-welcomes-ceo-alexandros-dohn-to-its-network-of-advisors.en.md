As part of this collaboration, Alexandros Dohn, CEO & Co-Founder of K2MATCH, joins the MCSC Group Network of Advisors, further strengthening the international expertise and multidisciplinary approach that define our ecosystem.

K2MATCH connects entrepreneurs with investors, corporates, business communities and expert solution partners while providing world-class growth solutions designed to accelerate innovation and sustainable business development.

This partnership reflects MCSC Group’s broader vision: creating meaningful connections between sport, business, investment and international advisory.

At MCSC, we increasingly observe that athletes, entrepreneurs and high-performing individuals face similar challenges — international environments, evolving opportunities, multiple stakeholders and the need for trusted strategic support. In this context, access to the right network becomes a decisive factor in creating long-term value.

Through this collaboration with K2MATCH, MCSC clients and partners will benefit from greater exposure to entrepreneurial ecosystems, investment communities and international business opportunities.

The addition of Alexandros Dohn to our Network of Advisors reinforces our commitment to surrounding our clients with experienced professionals capable of supporting growth, expansion and strategic decision-making across industries and geographies.

We look forward to working together and creating new opportunities through the combination of expertise, competencies and international vision.
