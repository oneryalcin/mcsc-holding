In April 2025, MCSC Group attended the Football Forum in Warsaw, an international gathering of football agents and players. The forum featured prominent figures such as Rafaela Pimenta and Roger Wittmann, offering insights into best practices and employment standards within the football industry.

Our participation in the forum highlighted our dedication to staying informed about the evolving landscape of football representation. Engaging with industry leaders provided us with valuable perspectives on player welfare, contract negotiations, and the importance of ethical representation. These insights are instrumental in refining our services to better support our clients' careers and uphold the highest standards of professionalism.
