MCSC Group was honoured to participate in the high-level diplomatic and economic event celebrating **20 years of diplomatic relations between the Principality of Monaco and the Republic of Slovenia**, held in Ljubljana.

Representing MCSC Group at the event was **Lada Alekseeva, Media Manager**, who joined business leaders, diplomats and institutional representatives to strengthen international connections and explore new opportunities for collaboration:

> — *The event brought together representatives of diplomatic institutions, business leaders and international stakeholders, creating an excellent platform for dialogue, collaboration and the development of new cross-border relationships.*
>
> *Throughout the day, our team had the opportunity to meet professionals from a wide range of industries, exchange perspectives on international business, and establish valuable new connections. As a company committed to supporting clients with international interests, MCSC Group believes that strong partnerships are built through meaningful conversations and long-term relationships.*
>
> *Participation in events such as this reflects our commitment to expanding our international network and strengthening cooperation between different business communities across Europe.*

We would like to express our sincere appreciation to everyone we had the pleasure of meeting and look forward to developing the relationships initiated during this event.

We are thankful to H.E. Ambassador Anne Eastwood and Stéphan Bruno for their kind invitation and warm hospitality.

At MCSC Group, we remain committed to fostering international dialogue and creating opportunities that connect business, expertise and trusted advisory services across borders.
