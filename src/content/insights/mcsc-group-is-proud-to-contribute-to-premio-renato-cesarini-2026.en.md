On May 25–26, the most prestigious last-minute goal award in Italian football **Premio Renato Cesarini** returns for its XI Edition at Hotel Federico II in Jesi, Italy.

Players from Serie A, B & C who scored decisive last-minute goals in the 2025/26 season will be honoured — alongside a special recognition for the heroes of Italy's 2006 World Cup triumph, marking 20 years since that unforgettable summer.

The event brings together some of the biggest names in Italian football history, top sports media figures, and distinguished guests from the worlds of business and diplomacy, for example Anne Eastwood, Ambassador of Monaco in Italy.

Alongside the award ceremony, the World Wide Business Summit offers two days of high-level networking for entrepreneurs, executives and professionals from Italy and beyond.

MCSC Group is honoured to contribute to an event where sporting excellence, business and culture converge.
