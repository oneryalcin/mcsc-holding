MCSC is pleased to announce its collaboration with Repeople Network.

Repeople Network was created with a clear mission: to facilitate meaningful connections between professionals operating in the world of real estate and alternative investments, while fostering strategic partnerships capable of generating long-term value in a responsible and sustainable environment.

This collaboration reflects a shared vision.

At MCSC, we believe that today’s international clients — whether in sports, business or investment environments — require more than isolated services. They require trusted ecosystems, cross-border expertise and access to high-level networks capable of supporting complex transitions and strategic growth.

Together with Repeople Network, our objective is to provide members and partners with privileged access to global expertise in asset structuring, career management and international advisory frameworks.

We are proud to begin this journey together and look forward to building meaningful opportunities through this partnership.
