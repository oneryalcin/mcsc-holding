We are thrilled to announce our official partnership with M3 Monaco — a leading force in smart & sustainable maritime development, headquartered in Monte Carlo.

M3 Monaco has built something remarkable: the S3 Standard (Monaco Smart & Sustainable Standard) — a global certification framework that raises the bar for marinas, yacht clubs, and sailing schools on sustainability, operational excellence, and environmental responsibility. Their work is anchored in the UN's 17 Sustainable Development Goals and pushes the maritime industry toward a cleaner, smarter future.

For MCSC, this collaboration means aligning with a partner that shares our commitment to excellence and long-term thinking. Together, we bring expertise, international networks, and a shared vision for what the maritime world can become.
