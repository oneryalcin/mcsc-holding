The 2023 Global Sports Summit, hosted by MCSC Holding in Monaco, brought together over 200 institutional investors, sports executives, and policy makers to discuss the future of investment in sports infrastructure.

## Summit Highlights

### Keynote: The Institutional Imperative

Marcus C. Sterling, Senior Founding Partner of MCSC Holding, opened the summit with a keynote address on the growing role of institutional capital in sports. "The professionalization of sports investment is no longer optional — it is an imperative," Sterling noted. "The governance structures we build today will define the industry for decades to come."

### Panel: Cross-Border Investment Structures

A panel featuring legal experts from Monaco, Milan, and Geneva explored the challenges and opportunities of cross-border sports investment. Key takeaways included:

- The importance of regulatory harmonization across EU jurisdictions
- Tax-efficient structuring for multi-territory sports assets
- The role of holding companies in providing governance frameworks

### Workshop: Due Diligence in Club Acquisitions

Elena Visconti, Managing Director of MCSC Italy, led a workshop on best practices for institutional due diligence when evaluating club acquisition opportunities. Participants worked through real-world case studies examining financial, legal, and reputational considerations.

## Looking Ahead: Monaco 2024

The success of this year's summit has set the stage for an expanded 2024 edition. MCSC Holding will host the next Global Sports Summit in September 2024, with an expanded focus on emerging markets and technology-driven sports infrastructure.
