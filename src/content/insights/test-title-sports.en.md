"I created the MCSC Group with friends, starting from a very simple idea. Based on my experience, the clients we support with our services and advice are generally highly complex and well‑structured, with diverse needs across different countries, situations, and specific contexts.

In my personal career, I've been working several years in banks, in family offices, and having become a sport agent, I thought with the changing environment, the service and the assistance to mainly sports people, entertainment people, and people around them, needs to be done differently. Today, what counts is the fact of being able to understand and to offer an assistance which is a 360 degrees assistance, mainly based on proven service providers, might it be internal to our group or external.

In this specific case, we work with partners and with service providers since many, many years, namely in my personal career, and their experience and track record is proven.

I consider in specific case the sports entertainment person like very similar to a family office client with complex needs, international diversified with several advisors and a timing factor that does not allow them to take care personally about their own needs. Here we come into the game, we understand their specific, we understand their way of living, we understand the fact that their career is very short to medium to long term but is very focused on the years of performance and will be totally different thereafter.

We think that we are in the position of being able to assist and to deliver the specific service required for each moment of the career of the sports and entertainment person."
